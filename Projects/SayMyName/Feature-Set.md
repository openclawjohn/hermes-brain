## Key Differentiators Summary

1. **WhatsApp sharing** — No competitor does this. Critical for SA market.
2. **Pronunciation guides** — Unique for African names. Hard to replicate.
3. **Multi-language UI** — English, Afrikaans, isiZulu. No competitor offers this.
4. **.co.za domain checking** — Unique to SA market.
5. **100% free, no signup** — Rare among competitors (most have premium tiers).
6. **SA language generators** — Zulu, Xhosa, Afrikaans, Sotho, Tswana, etc.
7. **Export (TXT/CSV)** — Most competitors don't offer this.
8. **Batch generation (50 names)** — Most competitors show 1-10 names.
